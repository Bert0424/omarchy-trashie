TRASHIE

Arrows / WASD to move · G targets · M mute · P pause · R restart

Hunter — BlinkyTargets Trashie's tile head-on.
Ambusher — PinkyTargets 4 tiles ahead of Trashie.
Flanker — InkyUses Hunter's position to pincer — vector from Blinky through 2 tiles ahead of Trashie, doubled.
Straggler — ClydeChases when far; peels off to his corner within 8 tiles.
Scatter / chase waves (7s / 20s …), forced reversals on every wave flip, frightened mode on the scan-shield pellet, eyes routing home through the door. Same maze every level (like the original) — but each level the bugs speed up, scatter phases shorten, and frightened time shrinks (gone by level 7). Maze layout is a first-pass string array to swap.
